## Removed / rejected ideas
- **Legacy's explicit hero "SOUND ON/OFF" toggle** — V2's persistent corner control already covers this need without adding a second, competing control to the hero.
- **Memory lightbox** — optional per the brief, and V2's editorial in-flow presentation already outperforms a gallery-style secondary interaction; adding one risks the exact "gallery app" feeling the brief warns against.
- **Utsubo's OffscreenCanvas/Worker rendering** — a real technique, not a content/interaction idea, and out of scope for a "no new architecture" pass (already noted and declined in the prior Utsubo pass).

## What actually changed this pass
One system only: **Journey (Chapter 04)**, which both the brief and direct
re-inspection confirmed was genuinely too lightweight relative to the rest of
the site. Everything else audited as already correct, already sourced from
the right place, or explicitly and reasoned-out as not worth adding.
